# cis-Pro sensitivity of reference strain

Poly-Pro at PPII preferred with **trans** ω: E_ref ≈ 325.  
Same with **cis** ω (0): E_ref ≈ 38.

Other sequences change less. Conclusions that lean on poly-Pro extremity must report both ω conventions.

On the AF-designed n=450 set, frac_gly predicts E_ref more than frac_pro (ρ +0.37 vs +0.17 pooled; within-N Pro mean ρ only +0.06) — consistent with design selection against extreme Pro obstruction under the generators used.
